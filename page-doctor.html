About the doctor

Dr. Yavuz Özsular was born in 1983 in Iğdır. He speaks fluent English and is happily married. He admires nature, forests, seas, and natural life. Dr. Yavuz Özsular enjoys tennis, trekking, squash, sailing, skiing, and football.

Educational Background

  • Dr. Yavuz Özsular completed his education at Gaziantep University Faculty of Medicine between 2001-2008.
  • During his university years, he was also interested in football and various sports branches and completed his education.

Professional Career

  • Between 2009-2013, Dr. Yavuz Özsular worked as a family doctor and assistant manager in the health directorate in Iğdır.
  • During this period, he worked as the provincial coordinator of the Family Medicine system and worked to establish the system in Iğdır.
  • During the same years, it organized training and seminars for many public institutions on infectious diseases and prevention methods. He received an award for this work.
  • After the Van earthquake in 2011, he voluntarily assumed the responsibility of Umke and worked actively.
  • Between 2013 and 2018, he completed his plastic reconstructive and aesthetic surgery residency at Yüzüncü Yıl University Faculty of Medicine.
  • During his residency, he performed many plastic surgeries including emergency traumas, hand surgery, tumor surgeries, congenital anomaly repairs, cleft palate, and lip surgeries as well as closed rhinoplasty.
  • While his residency training continued, he participated in surgeries as an observer besides Dr. Bülent Cihantimur, Dr. Ali Murat Akkuş, Dr. Barış Çakır.
  • Dr. Yavuz Özsular started his compulsory service in Bingöl State Hospital as of November 2018, and many trauma and burn patients have been followed up during his tenure.
  • He has been involved in rhinoplasty, eyelid surgeries, hand surgery operations, scoop ear surgeries, skin tumors surgery, breast augmentation-reduction, tummy tuck, liposuction.
  • From July 2020 to the present, Dr. Yavuz Özsular started to work in the world-renowned Estetik International Hospital.
  • In 2021 Dr. Yavuz started to work in Dr. Soho Clinic as the main plastic surgeon.

Courses & Seminars

Dr. Yavuz Özsular gave importance to his professional development and participated in many scientific congresses, symposiums and courses during his assistantship years. Enlisted below are a few examples of courses he's attented:
