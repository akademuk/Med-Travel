About Quironsalud Teknon

Teknon Medical Center is a part of Quironsalud Group, which opened its doors on February 18, 1994. The hospital is made on more than 70,000 m2 area and has provided care to more than 1,500,000 patients in its 30 years of history.

Annually more than 6,000 patients from 24 countries trust Quironsalud Teknon with their health. Commitment to excellence, use of the latest diagnostic technologies, and top-rated specialties are a few features that make Teknon the top priority of patients.

In addition to these multilingual services, a 24/7 contact center and a personalized treatment program are a few other highlight services.

Teknon has been awarded the gold seal of quality "JCI" and the "EFQM" accreditations for outstanding quality management. Due to patient satisfaction and treatment, the Medical Center is one of the few in Spain to acquire these certificates.

In today's medicine, research is an essential component and Teknon conducts research to contribute to the development of more effective therapies that improve healthcare and the quality of life of patients. The hospital has 77 ongoing clinical trials.

Infrastructure

  • 271 beds in the hospitalization area
  • 211 single rooms
  • 20 operating theatres
  • 21 ICUs
  • 150 doctors’ offices

  • 720 parking spaces

Milestones of Teknon Medical Center

Teknon Medical Center has consistently led the way in medical advancements, setting new standards in healthcare both in Spain and internationally. Below are some of the center's most notable milestones:

  • Performed the first e-PIPAC procedure in Catalonia.
  • Hosted the first Exchange Meeting in Breast Cancer Management, organized by the International Breast Cancer Center (IBCC).
  • Conducted the first mucosal defect closure interventions in Spain, led by the Teknon Digestive System Institute and Dr. Hugo Uchima.
  • Became the first private center in Catalonia to acquire the Da Vinci robot in 2011.
  • Installed the first high-field open MRI in 2012.
  • Installed a hybrid operating room in 2017.
  • The Digestive Endoscopy Unit performed the first stomach reductions in Spain using endoscopic methods without incisions.
  • Introduced the new BLI endoscopy, the first in Spain, enhancing early detection of esophageal and colon cancer.
  • Began using cell therapy to treat arthritis in 2013.
  • Installed a unique panoramic MRI system for high-quality diagnostics.
  • Pioneered bloodless surgery services in Spain, reducing infection risks by avoiding the use of donor blood.

Services for Patients at Teknon Medical Center

Teknon Medical Center offers a wide range of services designed to provide patients with a comfortable and supportive experience during their stay. These services include:

Hospital Accommodation

Patients can choose between suite-style and standard rooms, both equipped with essential amenities. The suite-style rooms include one bed, three sofas, two televisions, a refrigerator, two bathrooms, and an anteroom for visitors. Standard rooms have one bed, one sofa, one television, and one bathroom. Internet access, national and international TV channels, and a daily press service are available. A companion bed can be provided upon request, and an in-house laundry service operates from 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m.

Personalized Attention

Teknon Medical Center provides personalized care through a dedicated team of professionals. Each patient is assigned a personal assistant to guide them from admission to discharge, offering assistance in multiple languages. A bellboy is available to escort patients to their rooms and assist with luggage, while a luggage storage facility is also available on the same floor for convenience.

Personal Care

Patients at Teknon can benefit from exclusive personal beauty services, including laser treatments, facial and body care, hair diagnosis and treatment, and a hairdressing service. The center offers a comprehensive range of treatments, such as facial peels, anti-cellulite treatments, reflexology, and more.

How do I prepare for a Rhinoplasty?

Approximately two weeks prior to your nose job in New York, you will have a comprehensive visit in the office. A complete list of what to do and what to avoid is reviewed. Prescriptions for medications to be taken post-op are given, and the pre and post-op instructions are reviewed. Any questions that remain can be answered during this visit. Routine laboratory testing is ordered. Obtaining a cool mist humidifier for your room before surgery is recommended, and is used for the first weeks after your Rhinoplasty procedure. It is important to be sun-smart (using UVA and UVB sun protection) prior to surgery.

  • Do not take aspirin, anti-inflammatory drugs, and herbal supplements for 2 weeks before surgery as these may increase bleeding.
  • If you smoke, you should stop smoking at least two weeks before and two weeks after surgery to promote better healing.
  • Avoid drinking alcohol for at least 48 hours before surgery as it can lead to complications with anesthesia and can increase swelling.
