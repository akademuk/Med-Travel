Why Consider Plastic Surgery Abroad?

World-Class Surgeons

Plastic Surgeons  in countries like South Korea, Turkey, and Thailand are internationally trained and members of respected bodies like ISAPS and ASPS.
For example, Dr. Ergin Er in Turkey has over 30 years of experience and more than 10,000 successful surgeries. These doctors are known for their precision, aesthetic sense, and experience with international patients.

Accredited Clinics

Plastic surgery clinics abroad meet international safety standards and are certified by organizations like JCI or ISO.
Well-known facilities such as JK Plastic Surgery Clinic (South Korea) and Bumrungrad International Hospital (Thailand) are examples of globally accredited centers that serve thousands of international patients each year.

Shorter Waiting Times

In countries like the UK or Canada, patients wait months for elective procedures. In contrast, international clinics schedule surgeries within one to three weeks, allowing faster treatment and flexible planning.

Access to Trendy or Specialized Procedures

Some countries lead in specific procedures. South Korea is popular for V-line facial contouring, while Turkey is a top spot for rhinoplasty and hair transplants. Going abroad lets you benefit from surgeons who perform these treatments every day.

How to Choose a Safe Plastic Surgery Clinic Abroad?

  • Choose clinics with international certifications like JCI or ISO to ensure safety and quality care.
  • Confirm the surgeon is board-certified and has experience in your procedure. Look for memberships in ISAPS, ASPS, or KSPRS.
  • Read patient reviews on Google or Trustpilot to learn about results, staff, and aftercare.
  • Ask for a clear price breakdown. Check if it includes surgery, anesthesia, tests, follow-ups, hotel, airport pickup, and interpreter services.
  • Book a virtual consultation to speak with the surgeon and ask questions before making any decisions.

Pre- and Post-Surgery Care Guide for Medical Tourists

Pre-Trip Preparation

  1. Get a health check-up to make sure you're fit for surgery. Take any needed vaccines also.
  2. Prepare your medical history, including allergies, past surgeries, and chronic conditions, to share with your surgeon.
  3. Get travel insurance; some plans offer coverage for medical treatments abroad.
  4. Book a nearby stay at a hotel close to the clinic. Many hospitals in Turkey and South Korea partner with nearby hotels to help you recover comfortably.

How do I prepare for a Rhinoplasty?

Approximately two weeks prior to your nose job in New York, you will have a comprehensive visit in the office. A complete list of what to do and what to avoid is reviewed. Prescriptions for medications to be taken post-op are given, and the pre and post-op instructions are reviewed. Any questions that remain can be answered during this visit. Routine laboratory testing is ordered. Obtaining a cool mist humidifier for your room before surgery is recommended, and is used for the first weeks after your Rhinoplasty procedure. It is important to be sun-smart (using UVA and UVB sun protection) prior to surgery.

  • Do not take aspirin, anti-inflammatory drugs, and herbal supplements for 2 weeks before surgery as these may increase bleeding.
  • If you smoke, you should stop smoking at least two weeks before and two weeks after surgery to promote better healing.
  • Avoid drinking alcohol for at least 48 hours before surgery as it can lead to complications with anesthesia and can increase swelling.
